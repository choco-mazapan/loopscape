Online services used while running (no account or API key; nothing about you is sent beyond the request itself):
  Open-Meteo weather and geocoding  https://open-meteo.com/   (weather for the city you typed)
  BigDataCloud reverse geocoding     https://www.bigdatacloud.com/  (only if you choose "Windows location")
  GitHub Releases                    https://github.com/choco-mazapan/loopscape/releases  (style downloads)
